UK tender alert services compared

Every public tender is free to read on official websites. Alert services charge for doing the searching for you, and for extras such as filtering, history and analysis. This page sets out what each option says it offers, so you can choose what suits your firm.

Prices and features are as published on each service's own website on 16 to 18 November 2026. They change, so check the service's site before you buy. We run Noticehawk, one of the services listed.

Two things to check when comparing. A "free option" can mean a free search you run yourself or free alerts matched to your business and sent to you; the table says which. And some services quote prices before VAT: where a price above says "plus VAT", a firm that is not VAT registered pays a fifth more.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • Does it cover the work you do? General services cover every sector, which means more filtering. A construction-only service shows less, but less of it is irrelevant.
  • Does it cover the places you work? Check it reads Find a Tender (which now carries smaller contracts too) and, if you work there, Public Contracts Scotland.
  • Will it help you decide what to bid for? A long list of matches is only useful if you can tell quickly which are worth your time.
  • How is it billed? Some services sell annual contracts; others are monthly. Check notice periods and renewal terms.
  • Do you need extras? Bid writing tools, decision-maker contacts and market reports suit firms with a bid team. A small contractor may only need the right tenders in good time.
