The service

Noticehawk sends email alerts about UK public-sector tenders for construction and building maintenance work, filtered by the regions and types of work you choose. Scores are our own guide to how well a tender may suit a smaller firm. They are not advice, and a high score is not a prediction that you will win.

Accuracy

We take tender information from official notices and check for new ones daily, but we cannot guarantee that every relevant tender is included or that every detail is correct or current. The official notice, linked from each alert, is the authority. Check it before you decide to bid.

Plans and payment

The Free plan costs nothing. Paid plans are billed in advance through Stripe, in pounds sterling, either monthly or for a year at the price of ten months. A yearly plan runs for the year paid for and renews each year until you cancel. Noticehawk is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added to the prices shown. You can cancel at any time from the billing link in your emails; your plan then runs to the end of the month or year you have paid for and does not renew. We do not refund part-months, except where the law requires.

Free trial. Every paid plan, monthly or yearly, starts with 14 days free. You give your card details when you start, and nothing is charged during the 14 days. We email you 3 days before the trial ends, saying what will be charged and when. If you cancel before the trial ends, from the billing link in your emails, you pay nothing and your account moves to the Free plan. If you do not cancel, your card is charged the plan price on the day the trial ends and the plan continues as above. One free trial per business.

The subcontract work board

Businesses can post construction and building maintenance work on our subcontract board free of charge. If you post, you confirm that you are acting for the company named, that the work is real, and that you are content for the post and the reply details you give to be published. A post is published once you confirm your email address. We review posts and may remove any post. Posts come down on the closing date you give, or sooner if you remove them.

Posts are written by the businesses named, not by us. We do not vet those businesses, their terms or their ability to pay, and we are not a party to anything agreed between a poster and a firm that replies. Make your own checks before pricing or starting work.

AI assist

AI assist lists what a bid is likely to need, drawn from the notice, any documents the buyer has published openly, and our reading of them. It is a starting point. The buyer's tender pack is the only complete statement of what is required, and you remain responsible for your bid.
